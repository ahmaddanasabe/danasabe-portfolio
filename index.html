I chose Computer Science because I love building things that solve real problems. I taught myself web development without a mentor, starting with HTML and CSS, then JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, and I kept going because I enjoy seeing an idea turn into something people can actually use.

Computer Science gives me the foundation behind the code, while building real projects and teaching others keeps me growing. My goal is to turn this into a career as a software developer and eventually build a software company that serves real clients and solves real problems.
